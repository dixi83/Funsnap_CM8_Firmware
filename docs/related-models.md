# Related models, rebrands and FCC material

## CM8 filing

FCC ID `2APVH-CM8` belongs to Shenzhen Funsnap Technology. The archive retains the six-page internal photo exhibit, a one-page model-difference declaration dated 24 February 2025 and the W2 BLE test report. These document the certification sample and its declared model family. [Filing page](https://fccid.io/2APVH-CM8) · [Local exhibit index](../documents/README.md)

The declaration lists CM8, CM8S, CM8 Pro, CM8 Plus, CM6, CM7 and CM9 as having the same PCB, circuit and internal structure for that application. This is manufacturer evidence about those filing samples. Production revisions and later products sharing a model name can differ. [Manufacturer declaration](../documents/fcc/CM8-model-difference-8078952.pdf)

## Commercial rebrands

VEVOR sells model CM8. Its user manual inspected during the original search identifies Shenzhen Funsnap Technology as manufacturer and uses the Capture 2 ecosystem. This supports a commercial rebrand relationship. PCB revision equality with the owner's unit has not been established. The manual's remote mirror returned HTTP 403 during archive assembly, so its source link is retained. [VEVOR product page](https://www.vevor.nl/handheld-gimbals-stabilisatoren-c_14010/vevor-gimbal-stabilisator-voor-smartphones-met-ai-aangedreven-tracking-fill-light-p_010368069229) · [VEVOR manual source](https://device.report/m/6a213fd28bad8f46d21553cf3eccb0e8c92bafafacb26d2c8f43dc4df7a1ba51.pdf)

The Gadnic GIM00013 manual references `CM8-XXXX` and Capture 2. This supports a likely rebrand, with identical boards and firmware remaining unverified. ## Later CM9

The later CM9 has its own FCC ID `2APVH-CM9` and differing hardware. Its internal photographs show a DY-B03-02-V3 module designation also present on the owner's CM8. The original investigation also noted a 7.4 V CM9 supply specification against the CM8 filing's 3.7 V battery specification. These are reasons to track complete hardware revisions separately. [CM9 internal photographs](../documents/comparison/CM9-internal-photos.pdf) · [CM9 test report source](https://device.report/m/417a5953306e8497c14d5a9899c1be2a79e0df7e75aac0d4f9a694d406318189.pdf)

The owner's CM8 panel and the earlier CM8 filing already differ in Bluetooth implementation. Shared module markings, similar mechanical appearance and a locally matching firmware function are evidence for further comparison. Cross-flashing compatibility remains untested.
